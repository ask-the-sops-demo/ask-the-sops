# After-Hours On-Call Pharmacist

> FICTIONAL TRAINING DOCUMENT. Written for a software workshop. Not clinical or regulatory guidance. Do not use in practice.

## 1. Purpose
This procedure makes sure a pharmacist can be reached outside opening hours for urgent questions from staff, long-term care homes and prescribers.

## 2. On-call schedule
Pharmacists take turns being on call for one week at a time, from Monday 18:00 to the following Monday 08:00. The schedule is posted in the staff room and in the shared calendar.

## 3. Response time
The on-call pharmacist answers or returns every call within 30 minutes.

## 4. What counts as urgent
Urgent calls include a patient who has run out of an essential medication, a suspected dispensing error, a building alarm or power failure affecting medication storage, and a refrigerator alarm. For a refrigerator alarm, follow SOP-CC-01.

## 5. Records
The on-call pharmacist records each call in the on-call log the next working day: the time, the caller, the issue and the action taken.

## 6. Résumé en français
Un pharmacien ou une pharmacienne est de garde en dehors des heures d'ouverture. La personne de garde répond à chaque appel dans un délai de 30 minutes. Les appels urgents comprennent une rupture de médicament essentiel, une erreur de délivrance soupçonnée et une alarme de réfrigérateur.
