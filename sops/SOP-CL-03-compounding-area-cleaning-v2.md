# Non-Sterile Compounding Area Cleaning

> FICTIONAL TRAINING DOCUMENT. Written for a software workshop. Not clinical or regulatory guidance. Do not use in practice.

## 1. Purpose
This procedure keeps the non-sterile compounding area clean, to prevent contamination of compounded products and cross-contamination between them.

## 2. Cleaning frequency
1. Work counter and balance: before and after each compounding session, and whenever a spill occurs.
2. Equipment such as mortars, spatulas and ointment mills: immediately after each use.
3. Shelves, sink and floor: once a week.
4. Walls and ceiling: every three months.

## 3. Cleaning products
Clean with the approved detergent, rinse with purified water, then disinfect with 70% isopropyl alcohol and let it air dry. Never mix cleaning products.

## 4. Spills of hazardous products
Use the hazardous spill kit stored under the compounding counter. Follow the instructions in the kit, and tell the pharmacist on duty.

## 5. Records
Record each cleaning in the electronic cleaning log, with the date, the time, what was cleaned and your initials. The pharmacist reviews the log every week.
