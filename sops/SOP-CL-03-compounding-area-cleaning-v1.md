# Non-Sterile Compounding Area Cleaning

> FICTIONAL TRAINING DOCUMENT. Written for a software workshop. Not clinical or regulatory guidance. Do not use in practice.
> RETIRED: replaced by version 2. Kept for the record only.

## 1. Purpose
This procedure keeps the non-sterile compounding area clean, to prevent contamination of compounded products.

## 2. Cleaning frequency
The work counter is cleaned at the end of each day. The floor and shelves are cleaned once a month.

## 3. Cleaning products
Use the general-purpose cleaner from the supply room, followed by a water rinse.

## 4. Records
Record each cleaning on the paper log on the compounding room door.
